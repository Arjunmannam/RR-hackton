Pressure rule: second-innings chase, no more than 60 legal balls left, and required rate ≥9; or ≤24 balls left and required rate ≥7; or at least 5 wickets down and required rate ≥7. State is measured before each delivery. Impact: batting runs minus 6 for a batter dismissal; bowling 6 per credited wicket minus bowler runs conceded. Baselines are matched on T20/T20I and innings phase, leave the scored match out, and shrink sparse personal rates toward the format-phase average using a 60-ball prior. Rankings describe the supplied data and depend on these modeling choices.
